Chair Buddy Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Chair Buddy is a game-logging app for basketball scoretable chairpersons, published by JR Photo and Video Pty Ltd (Adelaide, South Australia). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Game records you enter: date, venue, competition, division, team names and colours, player numbers and names, coach and table-official names, fouls, timeouts, possession, quarter scores and protests.
  • Team rosters you choose to save to the shared database (team name, division, colours, coach names, player numbers and names).

Chair Buddy does not collect location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics, and contains no advertising or third-party tracking.

How it is used and shared

Chair Buddy is a shared system for approved scoretable chairs. Games and team rosters saved to the cloud are visible to, and can be updated by, other approved Chair Buddy users so that results and rosters can be shared between chairs. Information is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else except as required by law.

If you use Chair Buddy without signing in, your games stay on your device only.

Where it is stored

Cloud data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and sign-in emails are delivered by Resend. A copy of your games is also kept on your device so the app works without an internet connection.

Retention and deletion

Game records are kept as an ongoing record of competition results. You can delete games you created from within the app. To have your account or any personal information removed, email support@chairbuddy.com.au and we will action it within 30 days.

Children

Chair Buddy is intended for adult table officials. Player names entered may include junior players; enter only what your competition requires for the official record.

Contact
